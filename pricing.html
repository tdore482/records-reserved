Questions

Asked before signing up.

What happens when the seven days end?

You choose a plan — Standard at K25,000, Pro at K50,000 or Enterprise at K75,000 a month. Your catalogue, customers, vouchers and history stay exactly as you left them. Nothing is deleted because a trial ended, and there is no automatic charge — a plan starts when you decide it starts.

Do I need a card or bank details to start?

No. The trial asks for nothing but an email address and a password of twelve characters or more. There is no automatic charge at the end of it — a plan is a decision you make, not a subscription you cancel.

Does it handle services and rentals, or only products?

All three, in one catalogue. A product is counted stock, a service is sold with no stock at all, and a rental is an asset tracked unit by unit through a full hire lifecycle — rate cards by hour, day, week or month, deposits, late fees and returns. Most businesses mix all three; so does the till.

Do I need special hardware — a till printer, a card machine?

No. It runs in the browser on a phone, a tablet or a desktop, and installs to the home screen like a native app. Take payment however you already take payment — cash, card, mobile money, bank transfer or credit — and record it at the terminal. Receipts go out by email or SMS.

When can I add staff?

On Pro and above. Invite by email, assign each person to the locations they work, and choose whether they are a cashier at the till or a manager over the catalogue. On Standard the only membership is the owner — and that boundary is enforced by the database, not by a hidden button.

How is my business data protected?

Every row of data carries your business identifier, and the database refuses to answer for anyone else — a missing or forged request context matches nothing, and the system fails closed. Checkout runs as a single atomic transaction so two tills cannot oversell the last unit between them.

What does Enterprise add beyond Pro?

Several businesses under one account — for groups, franchises or sister companies — with switching between them in the top bar, plus subscription management, account reviews and a direct support line. Talk to us and we will provision it with you.

Which plan should I pick?

Standard — K25,000 a month — if it is you on the floor and you want the full till, catalogue and reports. Pro — K50,000 — the moment a cashier or manager needs their own login. Enterprise — K75,000 — when several businesses run under one owner. Unsure? Start the seven days on any plan and ask us — usually answered the same working day.
