Features

Every module answers to the same ledger.

The till, the catalogue, the hire desk, the voucher drawer and the reports are not five systems bolted together. They are one record, viewed from five angles — which is why the numbers agree at the end of the day.

Try every module free for 7 days Browse the modules

01 · Terminal

The till

Where the day is actually earned.

Pick the location, search the catalogue by name or SKU, tap items into the cart, adjust quantities, attach a customer or record a walk-in. Apply a voucher code and the discount is validated on the spot. Choose cash, card, mobile money, bank transfer or credit, then complete — one atomic step that locks stock, writes the sale and burns the voucher together.

  • Atomic checkout — no oversell, even across two tills
  • Five payment methods: cash, card, mobile, bank, credit
  • Customer attach or walk-in, with new-customer capture at the counter
  • Voucher codes validated and redeemed in the same transaction
  • Receipts delivered by email or SMS when contact details exist
  • Out-of-stock items dimmed before a cashier can reach for them

02 · Catalogue

Products, services and rentals

One list. Three kinds of line.

Every catalogue line declares what it is. A product is counted stock at each location. A service is sold with no stock row at all. A rentable is an asset tracked unit by unit through the hire lifecycle. The catalogue knows the difference, so the reports do too.

  • SKUs generated for you, or bring your own
  • Categories and units suggested from your business type
  • Add your own categories and units at any time
  • Per-location quantities with low-stock thresholds
  • Soft delete that keeps sales history intact
  • Images stored with the item for quick visual picking

03 · Hire

The rental lifecycle

From the rate card to the return desk.

Rentable items carry a rate card — hourly, daily, weekly and monthly — plus a minimum billing period, a security deposit and a late fee. Each physical asset is a tracked unit with its own status. A booking takes a unit out; a return settles it, applies late and damage charges against the deposit, and books the revenue when the elapsed time is known.

  • Unit statuses: available, reserved, on hire, maintenance, lost
  • Booking statuses: reserved, checked out, returned, cancelled
  • Deposits held as liability — never counted as revenue
  • Late and damage charges settled from the deposit at return
  • Revenue recognised at return, matching how hire is actually billed
  • On-hire counts reconstructable from the stock movement ledger

04 · Customers

Loyalty that runs itself

Visits and spend counted without asking.

Attach a customer to a sale and the database updates their visit count and lifetime spend immediately. Tiers recompute on the same write: bronze after three visits or K25,000, silver after eight or K100,000, gold after fifteen or K350,000. Crossing a threshold mints a 10% voucher valid for thirty days, waiting in their name at the next visit.

  • Automatic visit and lifetime-spend tracking
  • Bronze, silver and gold tiers with configurable thresholds
  • Auto-issued 10% vouchers on every tier-up, valid 30 days
  • Customer directory with tier badges and active coupons
  • Daily promotion runs that target eligible customers with contact details
  • Cooldown windows so nobody is spammed with the same offer

05 · Vouchers

Coupons with discipline

Single-use, scoped and expiring by design.

Percent or fixed discounts. Assigned to a named customer or open to anyone. Scoped to a location or redeemable anywhere in the business. Every voucher has an expiry and burns to redeemed the moment it is used — a code cannot be replayed, even if two tills try at once.

  • Percent or fixed discount types
  • Customer-assigned or open codes
  • Location-scoped or business-wide redemption
  • Single-use enforcement at checkout, not at display time
  • Loyalty, promotion and manual issue sources
  • Status history: active, redeemed, expired, cancelled

06 · Money

Expenses, credit and balance

The other half of the day.

A till that only counts income tells half the story. Log cash-out expenses by category and location so revenue against expense is a real number. Credit sales — recorded as their own payment method — separate what you have been paid from what you are still owed.

  • Expense capture by category and location
  • Voidable expense entries with attribution
  • Revenue-versus-expense net balance in reports
  • Credit sales tracked and reported apart from cash
  • Expense breakdown by category and by store
  • Everything feeds the same dashboard the till feeds

07 · Reports

Numbers you can reconcile

Built from the ledger, not from a cache.

Sales by period with daily, weekly and monthly views. Revenue by location. Top sellers. A dedicated expenses report. And a catalogue report that reconstructs opening stock, stock in, units sold, manual adjustments and closing stock for every item over any period — with a closing inventory value attached.

  • Sales report by day, week and month, per location or whole business
  • Revenue by store with visual comparison
  • Top sellers over any period
  • Revenue versus expenses and net balance
  • Expense breakdown by category and by store
  • Catalogue report: opening, stock in, sold, adjustments, closing, value

08 · People

Staff and boundaries

Everyone gets exactly what their job needs.

Invite staff by email and place them at the locations they work. Cashiers run the till and manage customers. Managers add catalogue edits, expenses and voucher issues. Owners hold settings, locations, staff roles — and can transfer ownership or reset the business when it changes hands. On the Standard plan the only membership is the owner; the database refuses the rest until you move up to Pro.

  • Email invitations with per-tenant roles
  • Per-location staff assignment
  • Cashier, manager and owner capability tiers
  • Passcode reset by the owner
  • Ownership transfer to another member
  • Business reset when a location needs a clean start

09 · Security

The boundary lives in the database

Not in a hidden menu.

Every tenant-scoped table carries a business identifier. Access is resolved from the signed-in session on the server — a missing or forged header matches zero rows, and the system fails closed. Sensitive writes run through server-side functions that re-check authorisation before anything is stored. A screen that does not render for a role is a courtesy; the refusal to return the data is the control.

  • Row-level security on every tenant-scoped table
  • Tenant resolution fails closed on missing or forged context
  • Checkout and administrative writes re-authorised server-side
  • Twelve-character password minimum with breach checking
  • Multi-factor authentication available for owners and managers
  • Platform administration kept entirely separate from business roles

10 · Everywhere

Installs like an app

The counter gets a till, not a tab.

The platform carries a web app manifest and install prompt. Add it to a phone or a desktop home screen and it opens full screen, without the browser furniture. Receipts go out by email and SMS through the messaging adapters — with a console fallback in development so nothing is ever silently dropped.

  • Installable on phone and desktop home screens
  • Opens full screen like a native application
  • Receipts by email and SMS where contact details exist
  • Server-side re-validation before any receipt is sent
  • Works on the counter tablet, the owner's phone, the office desktop
  • No special hardware, no till printer required to begin
